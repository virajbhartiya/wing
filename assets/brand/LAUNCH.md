## Single post

Introducing Wing, an open-source browser for Mac.

Spaces, split view and Peek, on a native Chromium engine. Chrome extensions install straight from the Web Store. Calls don't freeze when you switch tabs, and you pick exactly what to share.

Free, and the code is yours to read.

https://github.com/virajbhartiya/wing

## Thread (optional replies)

2/ Spaces keep work, side projects and reading apart. Each one has its own pinned tabs and its own look.

3/ Split view puts two pages side by side. Peek opens a link on top of the page you're on, without losing your place.

4/ Google Meet, Zoom and Teams stay live in the background. When a site asks to share your screen, Wing asks first: one tab, one window or the whole screen.
